FAQ

Good questions.

Are the tools free?

Yes. Everything runs in your browser — there is no server and no subscription.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no sign-in — the tools run inside your own browser tab, and nothing is uploaded.

Which stores are covered?

Flipkart Minutes, Blinkit, Amazon Now, Swiggy Instamart, BigBasket, Zepto, JioMart, Myntra and Croma — nine stores in total, plus the all-stores hub.
